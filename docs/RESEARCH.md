# Research: what high-converting e-book and digital-product landers do

Done on 18 November 2026, before and during the build. We adopted the patterns below. We did not copy any copy or assets.

## Pages and sources studied

| Source | What we took from it |
|---|---|
| MAKE, readmake.com (Pieter Levels) | A one-line proof strip above the headline. The proof figure sits directly under the main CTA. A huge display headline, then a short benefit line. |
| Refactoring UI, refactoringui.com | A dark gradient hero with faint perspective grid lines. The 3D book is angled and lit, and bleeds into the background with no box. A credentials eyebrow row. Two CTAs: a free sample (primary) and "Buy now" (secondary). A "what we've put together" contents section. Price tiers in a single, high-contrast pricing block. The FAQ comes last. |
| Building a Second Brain, buildingasecondbrain.com/book | A book mockup with a soft contact shadow. A brand line motif runs out from the book into the background. One strong, high-contrast CTA with a bonus line under it. |
| Ali Abdaal's Part-Time YouTuber Academy (SchoolMaker breakdown, schoolmaker.com/breakdowns/ptya) | A clean, simple hero. An honest, non-pushy tone, and "secret" framing avoided. |
| Justin Welsh essays (justinwelsh.me/essays/4-tips-to-convert-visitors-into-sales and /fix-your-broken-sales-page) | Proof above the fold. Make buying effortless. Specific numbers beat vague claims. "Curtain" language to tease what's inside. |
| Course Coach, "12-section formula" (course-coach.pages.dev) | Section order. A sticky CTA bar after the hero on long pages. Repeat the CTA after the FAQ and at the bottom. |
| Thrive Themes, Kajabi, SchoolMaker sales-page guides | 5–10 FAQs that remove objections. A guarantee framed as risk reversal. A value stack in the pricing card. |
| Apexure, "ebook landing page" (apexure.com/ebook-landing-page) | A 3D mockup beat a flat cover in their A/B tests. Show real page previews. |
| SeedProd, Unbounce, AppSumo e-book example roundups | A single-column, benefit-led hero with a 3D cover. Colour-coded section rhythm. |

## Patterns we adopted

1. **Section order:** proof strip → hero → product in action (our film) → problem and promise → proof (our research) → what's inside (chapters plus page previews) → who it's for and not for → free sample → pricing → FAQ → final CTA.
2. **Hero:** a display headline in the e-book's Anton type, then the benefit in one sentence, then specific counts (82 pages, 9 chapters, 50 concepts, 6 templates). The primary CTA shows the price inside the button, with the secondary "free sample" next to it. Trust micro-copy sits under the buttons.
3. **Product visual:** a 3D book rendered on a transparent background (no rectangle), with a contact shadow and a soft cyan ambient glow that matches the page. Real page previews fan out behind it. A thin cyan stair-line motif, taken from the Linewise logo, runs through the background.
4. **Proof:** honest only. We show the research numbers (16 films with 3–29M views each, 160+ Shorts measured), each with its source and a clear "these are other creators' films" note. There are no testimonials or sales counts until real ones exist.
5. **Pricing:** one price in a single high-contrast card, with a value stack and the risk-reversal line. **There is no fake "was £X" anchor**, because UK pricing law (CMA guidance and DMCC Act 2024) treats invented reference prices as misleading.
6. **CTAs:** the same primary CTA appears four times (hero, pricing, final, header). A sticky buy bar shows on phones after the hero scrolls away and hides while the pricing card is on screen.
7. **FAQ:** 10 questions that remove objections, also published as FAQPage JSON-LD.
8. **Mobile-first:** the headline and CTA come first, with the product visual under them. Tap targets are at least 48 px. There is no horizontal scroll, and nothing is hidden behind hover.
